Balakrishnaa Textiles has showrooms in Selaiyur (two branches) and Tambaram with women's, men's and kids' clothing under one roof.

All three showrooms are open daily from 9:30 AM to 10:00 PM, as listed on our contact page.

Yes. The collection covers sarees, kurtis, salwar and churidar sets, unstitched dress materials, men's shirts and trousers, and kids' wear.

Yes, family fitting suites are available in store, so you can check size and fit in person.
