Heritage & Destination Retail

Welcome to Balakrishnaa Stores

Balakrishnaa Stores is a clothing and fashion store located in Selaiyur, Chennai, offering a comprehensive variety of clothing collections for women, men, and children.

Spanning dedicated multi-floor halls on Velachery Main Road, our showroom invites families to comfortably browse handpicked cottons, breezy organzas, festive georgettes, structured linens, and handcrafted weaves. Customers can touch and feel pure textile craftsmanship and experience unhurried personalized in-store trials inside spacious, air-conditioned fitting rooms.
